Dialysis stock consumption rules.

General Consumption

Each dialysis patient normally uses:

  • 1 Dialyzer
  • 1 Blood Lines
  • 1 Bicarbonate (Part-B)
  • 1 Syringe 10CC
  • 1 Acid (Part-A)
  • 2 IV Sets
  • 2 Normal Saline
  • 6 pairs Latex Gloves

AVF Patient

AVF patients additionally use:

  • 1 AVF Needle
  • 1 Syringe 5CC

Catheter Patient

Catheter patients use:

  • 2 Syringe 5CC
  • 1 additional IV Set
  • 2 Sterile Gloves

New Catheter Patient

A new catheter patient additionally uses:

  • 1 Temporary Catheter

Medication

  • 1 EPO for each applicable patient
  • Iron according to clinical requirement
  • Heparin according to dialysis protocol
